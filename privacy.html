MISTFALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

MISTFALL is a free, single-player strategy game made by Kyle Alander. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no sign-in, no tracking and no data sent to the developer.

Your saved worlds

Your worlds and settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to export a world yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Internet use

The game can be played offline. When your device is online, the app loads the game and checks for updates from GitHub Pages (kylealander-crypto.github.io) and the GitHub API (api.github.com). These requests contain no personal information from the game. GitHub, like any web host, may record standard technical information such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/kylealander-crypto/Mistfall/issues.
